Folder 03 · A Calendar in Four Dyes

Clementine's
hair is a
timeline.

Clementine Kruczynski dyes her hair on impulse, and because this is a film that moves backwards, her hair becomes the only reliable way to tell where in the relationship you are. Four colors. Four phases. One love affair.

Phase 01Green Revolution Phase 02Red Menace Phase 03Agent Orange Phase 04Blessed Blue

Read the timeline forwards.

In the film, Clementine's hair gets younger as the movie plays — we meet blue first, then orange, then red, then green. Chronologically, though, their relationship begins in green and ends in orange. Blue is the second first time.

Phase 01 · The Beginning

Green
Revolution

The dye Clementine is wearing when Joel, in his own story, actually meets her for the first time.

A beach party on Long Island. A friend of a friend. She has deep, almost emerald-green hair and a half-finished drink and she sits down next to him because she has decided he is interesting. This is the memory Joel goes looking for during the erasure — the one before the one before the one. The sweetest, and therefore the last to be deleted, and therefore the hardest to let go of.

Green is the color of their beginning. It is also the color a person dyes their hair when they want to seem reckless but are secretly very careful — a shade that says I am not a person who will be mistaken for anyone normal.

Phase 02 · The Honeymoon

Red
Menace

The happy middle. The shade she's wearing on the frozen river under the stars — and on the kitchen floor making pasta at 1 a.m.

Red is the phase in which Joel says the words I could die right now, Clem. I'm just… happy. It is also the phase in which he fails to say that he means it. Every couple gets a red-hair phase. It lasts, on average, four to eleven months.

The naming is Clementine's own: she catalogues her dye jobs by what she's reacting to when she does them. Red Menace is what comes after Green Revolution, when she is no longer introducing herself to someone and is instead announcing, to her friends, that she is in love.

Phase 03 · The Unravelling

Agent
Orange

The color of disappointment. The color of a fight in a Chinese restaurant that neither of them started.

Agent Orange is the phase where Clementine asks, across a basket of dumplings, whether they have become "the dining dead." It is the phase where Joel stops writing in his journal. It is the phase where she gets home drunk at 3 a.m. and he pretends to be asleep. It is the phase that ends, more or less, when she walks into Lacuna, Inc. and says she would like to pay them to make all of this stop.

Orange is on her head during every single fight we are shown. This is not an accident. The dye job is itself an act of hostility: she changes her hair because she knows he didn't like the red.

Phase 04 · The Second Beginning

Blessed
Blue

The shade she is wearing the morning she walks back into Joel's life without knowing she has ever been there before.

Blue is the color of the Lacuna era. She dyes her hair in the days immediately after the procedure, because she has the vague sense — where does it come from? — that she has just done something new. She takes the train to Montauk because she has the vague sense she is supposed to. She has an orange hoodie on. The hoodie is the last surviving trace of the last phase.

Blessed Blue is the color on her head for the entire opening act of the film — the parts that play as a meet-cute and are actually, tragically, a meet-again. It is also the color she is wearing at the end, when she stands in Joel's hallway and listens to a cassette of him calling her small and insecure, and decides, despite everything, to say okay.

  • The Montauk beach, opening

The colors loop. Green, red, orange, blue — and then, eventually, green again. Clementine has dyed her hair this way before she met Joel and she will dye it this way after she forgets him. The film's final shot — the two of them running on the beach at Montauk, over and over — is, if you look, always a woman with blue hair. The cycle is starting. It has already started. It will start again.
